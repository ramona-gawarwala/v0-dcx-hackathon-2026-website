# Judging

## How it works

- Each team gives a **[2–3] minute demo** of the working app (not slides).
- Judges score against the criteria below.
- Multiple awards — beginners and non-engineers can win too.

## Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | Question |
|---|---:|---|
| Impact | 30% | Does it solve a real problem worth solving? |
| Innovation | 25% | Is it creative or a fresh use of AI? |
| Execution | 25% | Does it actually work in the demo? |
| Experience | 10% | Is it clear and pleasant to use? |
| Demo | 10% | Was the pitch clear and to the point? |

Weights are a guide — adjust to your event goal.

## Awards

- Best Overall
- Best Use of AI
- Best Design
- Best First-Timer / Beginner
- [Add your own]

## What judges reward

Working demos, creativity, and clear thinking — not polish or startup pitches. You don't need a finished product to win.
