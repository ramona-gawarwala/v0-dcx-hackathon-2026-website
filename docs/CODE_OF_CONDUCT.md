# Code of Conduct

This hackathon is for everyone — every role, every skill level. We're here to build, learn, and have fun, and we want everyone to feel safe, respected, and included.

As a Capgemini event, we hold ourselves to the same standard as our everyday work: our seven Values and our Code of Business Ethics guide how we treat each other here.

## Grounded in our Values

Capgemini's seven Values shape this hackathon:

- **Honesty** — be open, represent your own work truthfully, and give credit where it's due.
- **Boldness** — take creative risks and share ideas freely.
- **Trust** — assume good intent and empower your teammates.
- **Freedom** — respect other people, cultures, and viewpoints.
- **Fun** — enjoy the build, and help others enjoy it too.
- **Modesty** — stay humble, listen, and lift others up.
- **Team spirit** — collaborate generously and share what you learn.

## Expected

- Be welcoming and patient — especially with first-timers.
- Be respectful of different skill levels, roles, and ideas.
- Make sure everyone is heard — take turns sharing ideas and invite quieter voices in.
- Listen actively, and give feedback that is kind, specific, and constructive.
- Use inclusive, plain language, and avoid jargon that leaves people out.
- Assume good intent, and be open to being corrected gracefully.
- Help others when you can, and give credit for their work.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Interrupting, talking over people, or dominating the conversation.
- Dismissing ideas, gatekeeping, or making anyone feel they don't belong.
- Disruptive, unsafe, or dishonest behavior.

## Reporting

Something felt off? Please tell us. You won't be overreacting, and you won't be causing trouble — if you see or experience harassment, feel unsafe, or something just doesn't sit right, we want to know so we can help.

We follow the same principles as Capgemini's SpeakUp ethics helpline: raise concerns in good faith, and we'll listen, investigate fairly, keep it confidential, and protect you from any form of retaliation. The form is anonymous — we don't record your name, and you only share your name and email if you'd like us to follow up.

For serious or work-related concerns, you can also use Capgemini's [SpeakUp](https://www.capgemini.com/speakup/) helpline, available 24×7.
