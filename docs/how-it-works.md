# How it works

## The basics

- **Teams:** 2–5 people. Solo is allowed but a team is more fun.
- **Duration:** 2 weeks — **1–14 September 2026**. Part-time: build around your day job, no need to drop everything.
- **Goal:** ship a working, demoable AI app. Done beats perfect.
- **Tools:** [v0](https://v0.dev) + [Vercel](https://vercel.com). Use any AI model available via the [AI Gateway](https://vercel.com/docs/ai-gateway).

## Schedule

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Mon 1 Sep | Kickoff + team formation |
| Week 1 (1–7 Sep) | Pick a challenge, start building |
| Throughout | Mentors available in [channel/room] |
| Week 2 (8–14 Sep) | Keep building, wrap up your demo |
| Sun 14 Sep, [time] | Submissions due — see [Submit](submission.md) |
| [Demo day, time] | Demos + judging — see [Judging](judging.md) |

## Forming a team

Don't have a team? Post in **[team-forming channel]** with one line: *what you want to build* and *what you bring* (dev, design, product, domain). Skim others' posts and reply to team up. At kickoff we'll also help group anyone still solo.

Mixed teams (not all engineers) tend to build better products.

## Not sure what to build?

Start by picking a [project type](project-types.md), then scope a [challenge](challenges.md). Aim to finish ~25% of your big idea — pick something small enough to demo.

Questions? See [Help](help.md).
