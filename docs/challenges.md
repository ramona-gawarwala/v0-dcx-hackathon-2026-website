# Challenges

Pick one. These are starting points, not rules — a scoped idea beats a big vague one.

Not sure what kind of thing to make? Start with [Project types](project-types.md).

Each challenge works for **any** project type — an app, an agent, a workflow, a dashboard, or an experience. It just needs to solve the problem and be demoable.

## Choose your level

Your level is about **how much the AI does for you** — not how technical it looks. A brilliant text-only tool can reach the top. Higher isn't required to win — a sharp 🌱 **Seed** beats a broken 🍂 **Harvest**. Call out your level at your demo.

| Level | Code word | How much the AI does | What it means |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 🌱 **Seed** | Answers or creates | You prompt it, it responds — answer, summarise, rewrite, generate. |
| 2 | 🌿 **Sprout** | Works with your stuff | It uses your documents, data, or a tool/API to do something real. |
| 3 | 🍂 **Harvest** | Does it for you | It takes several steps on its own — an agent, a workflow, or a chain. |

Start where you're comfortable and climb if you have time.

### Add a flavour (optional)

Modality — **voice, images, or video** — is a flavour you can add at *any* level, not a level of its own. It's a great way to stand out:

- 🎙️ **Voice** — speak to it (speech-to-text) or have it speak back (text-to-speech).
- 🖼️ **Images** — read a photo, screenshot, or diagram (image-to-text).
- 🎬 **Video** — pull a summary or key moments from a clip (video-to-text).

A text-only 🌿 Sprout with a voice input is just as impressive as a bigger build — pick what serves your idea.

### See it in practice

The same idea grows as the AI does more. Take a **language-learning helper**:

| Level | What it does | The AI is… |
|---|---|---|
| 🌱 Seed | Generates example sentences from a word | …creating on request |
| 🌿 Sprout | Quizzes you on *your* vocab list | …working with your data |
| 🍂 Harvest | Plans a lesson, quizzes you, tracks progress | …running several steps for you |

Add a flavour to any row — e.g. speak your answers aloud (voice) — for extra stretch.

## 🌱 Seed — AI answers or creates

You prompt it, it responds. The fastest way to a working demo, and perfect for first-timers. A sharp text tool belongs here proudly.

**Rewrite Helper**
**Problem:** a message, summary, or note takes too long to get right.
**Build:** an assistant that summarises, rewrites, translates, or reformats text.
**Done when:**
- [ ] It does its one job on a real example.
- [ ] Anyone can use it without instructions.

**Draft It**
**Problem:** starting from a blank page is the hardest part.
**Build:** generate a first draft — an email, a plan, a snippet — from a short brief.
**Done when:**
- [ ] It turns a one-line brief into something usable.
- [ ] The result is a real head-start, not just filler.

## 🌿 Sprout — AI works with your stuff

Give the AI your documents, data, or a tool so it does something real — not just chat.

**Ask My Docs**
**Problem:** the answer is buried in documents nobody wants to read.
**Build:** point it at your own docs and ask questions grounded in them.
**Done when:**
- [ ] It answers from your content, not general knowledge.
- [ ] It says "I don't know" instead of making things up.

**Live Lookup**
**Problem:** a useful answer needs fresh or private data the model doesn't have.
**Build:** give the AI a tool — an API, a search, a database — it can call to fetch what it needs.
**Done when:**
- [ ] It calls the tool and uses the result in its answer.
- [ ] It works on a real, current example.

## 🍂 Harvest — AI does it for you

Multi-step and mostly hands-off: an agent or workflow that reaches a result on its own. What makes it an *agent* — not just a chatbot — is two things working together:

- **A reasoning loop** — it observes, plans, acts, then checks the result and repeats until the goal is met.
- **Tools** — the actions it can take: search the web, query a database, call an API, send an email.

The loop is the *thinking*; the tools are the *hands*. Reasoning with no tools can only talk; tools with no reasoning can only follow a fixed script. Together, the AI picks a tool, uses it, reads what comes back, and decides the next step.

**Do It For Me**
**Problem:** a repetitive, multi-step task eats time that could go elsewhere.
**Build:** an agent or workflow that completes the task end to end.
**Done when:**
- [ ] It runs end to end on a real example.
- [ ] A human only steps in for the tricky bits.
- [ ] It handles at least one obvious edge case gracefully.

**The Team Play**
**Problem:** one assistant isn't enough — the task needs different skills or steps.
**Build:** a workflow or multiple agents that collaborate toward a result (see the Multi-Agent [project type](project-types.md)).
**Done when:**
- [ ] Each step or agent has a clear job.
- [ ] They combine into one coherent result you can demo.

## Tips that make it land

- **Pick a problem worth solving.** Go for a task that's slow *and* painful, not just frequent — a high-effort job often beats a high-volume trivial one. That's where the impact is.
- **Plan for when the AI is unsure.** Decide what happens if it can't answer: say "I don't know", offer a fallback, or hand off to a human. Don't let it guess.
- **Use data responsibly.** Demo with dummy or public data — never paste real customer data, secrets, or personal information into prompts or logs.
