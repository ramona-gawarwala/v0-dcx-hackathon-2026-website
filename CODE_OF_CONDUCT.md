# Code of Conduct

This hackathon is for everyone. We're here to build, learn, and have fun.

## Expected

- Be welcoming and patient — especially with first-timers.
- Be respectful of different skill levels, roles, and ideas.
- Help others when you can.

## Not tolerated

- Harassment, discrimination, or demeaning comments.
- Disruptive or unsafe behavior.

## Reporting

Contact [organizer name / email / channel]. Reports are handled confidentially.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
